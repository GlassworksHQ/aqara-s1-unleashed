(public domain), from Wikimedia Commons. `make.py` makes them from each photo's 1280 px preview
(checked by SHA-256): cropped to 16:9 from the top, anything readable blurred, graded to dusk (the
dashboard shows 19:30; darker, flatter pictures also keep the renders under 80 KiB), 800×450 JPEG.
