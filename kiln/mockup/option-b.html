Step 1 · Browser

Turn your S1 Plus into a Home Assistant kiosk

Install S1 Kiosk over one USB cable, right from this page. The panel's own software is backed up first, so it can all be undone. About 10 minutes.

WebUSB supportedSecure connectionChrome on macOS
macOS Nothing to install.
Windows One-time Zadig step: give 1f3a:efe8 the WinUSB driver. We'll walk you through it.
Linux Add a udev rule so you can open the panel without root.

Step 2 · FEL mode

Hold the button. Plug in. Keep holding.

Unplug the panel and take off the back cover. The button is the small one on the board, below the round sensor window, not the reset button on the case.

01Hold the buttonPress and keep it down.
02Plug in USB-CInto this computer, still holding.
10 sKeep holdingThen let go. The screen stays black.
Pick USB Device (1f3a:efe8) when asked
